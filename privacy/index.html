Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Pincio Insights handles data when a creator authorizes access to a TikTok account. Pincio Insights is a private analytics tool used to analyze authorized TikTok account data and prepare private reports for the creator.

2. Data accessed

Pincio Insights accesses only TikTok account and video data that the creator authorizes through TikTok's official OAuth process and APIs. The specific data available depends on the permissions granted and on the fields made available by TikTok.

3. How data is used

Authorized TikTok data is used only to calculate analytics, compare video performance over time, and prepare private reports for the creator. Pincio Insights does not use this access to publish, modify, or delete content on TikTok.

4. Sale and access

Pincio Insights does not sell personal data. Access to the data is limited to the operator of Pincio Insights and the tools used to operate the private analytics and reporting service.

5. Revoking authorization

You may revoke Pincio Insights' access through the authorization controls provided by TikTok. Revoking access prevents future API access but does not itself delete data that was previously obtained.

6. Deletion requests

You may request deletion of data associated with your authorized TikTok account by contacting us at the address below.

7. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the operation of Pincio Insights changes. The effective date above will be updated when a revised version is published.

8. Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests may be sent to alessiomascolo.mgmt@gmail.com.
